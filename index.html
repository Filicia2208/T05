TV Power Consumption

This dashboard explores television energy consumption and Australian electricity price trends using four interactive data visualisations. Compare energy-efficiency ratings with electricity consumption, examine differences between screen technologies, and explore how average spot electricity prices have changed over time.

Energy Consumption vs Star Rating

Each point represents a listed television model. Compare its labelled annual energy use with its efficiency star rating; colours identify screen technology.

Energy Consumption by Screen Technology

Mean annual energy consumption across all screen sizes. Slice size shows the relative mean for each technology.

Energy Consumption of 55-inch TVs

Compare the mean labelled annual energy consumption for LCD, LED, and OLED televisions in the 55-inch dataset.

Australian Spot Power Prices (1998–2024)

The line tracks the supplied yearly average spot electricity price, excluding Tasmania and Snowy, in dollars per megawatt hour.
